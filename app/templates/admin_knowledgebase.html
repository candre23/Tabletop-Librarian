Scanned Document OCR

Completing OCR marks the knowledgebase for update but does not start that CPU-intensive update automatically.

This cleanup removes abandoned, obsolete, and orphaned CBR extraction caches while preserving fallback caches for known offline library sources. OCR PDFs and knowledgebase data are not removed.
